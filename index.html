Ticketing system · proof of concept

A ticket can reach someone on WhatsApp or in Teams.

Status changes and SLA clocks become notifications. Each tenant, and each user, decides whether that notification is sent and which medium carries it. A WhatsApp reply can return to the ticket as a comment, or continue the conversation.

01 · The scenario

The ticketing system keeps the ticket. A notification step carries the news.

  1. 1

    Did this action happen?

    Someone changed a ticket’s status, or an SLA clock reached a point that should be announced.

  2. 2

    Should this person hear it?

    The tenant can turn the notification on or off. Each user can turn it off for themselves.

  3. 3

    WhatsApp or Teams?

    The medium is a setting. The same action can travel on either channel.

06 · Integration steps

The steps, briefly.

WhatsApp

  1. Create a Meta app and add the WhatsApp product.
  2. Configure the phone number ID, the access token, and the webhook verify token.
  3. Send the notification from the ticketing system through that API.
  4. Publish one webhook for inbound messages and delivery status.
  5. Submit business verification, then register the company number.
  6. Issue a lasting token and send with approved templates.

Teams

  1. Register a single-tenant application in Microsoft Entra ID.
  2. Grant admin consent for Chat.Create, ChatMessage.Send, and User.ReadBasic.All.
  3. Create a sender account with a Teams license and sign in once.
  4. Resolve the person by work email, open the 1:1 chat, and post the message.
  5. Select Teams in the same notification step when that medium is configured.

07 · Meta business verification

Six documents. An admin uploads them.

Meta recommends this set. Use copies that show the legal business name. The review window is 5 to 10 days after a complete submission. The proof of concept can stay on the test number during the review.

  1. 01

    Certificate of Incorporation

    Signed, stamped, sealed, or e-filed. Add a Certificate of Filing if you have one.

  2. 02

    Business license

    Issued by a government authority.

  3. 03

    Tax registration

    GST, VAT, or EIN, issued by a tax agency.

  4. 04

    Bank statements

    The latest statements.

  5. 05

    Utility bills

    Electricity, gas, water, internet, or phone. The latest bills.

  6. 06

    Credit report

    From a recognized reporting agency.

  • Clear, full pages only. Expired, blurry, cropped, or incomplete files are rejected.
  • Leave QR codes and barcodes in place.
  • A bilingual copy helps when a document is not in English.
  • Only an admin can complete business verification.

08 · Meta’s charges

Meta bills a delivered message. There is no monthly WhatsApp API subscription in this design.

Rates are for a recipient on country code +94, from the card effective 1 October 2026. Amounts are US dollars. The country of the sender does not set the rate. A message that is not delivered is not charged.

Marketing0.0732

Offers, launches, re-engagement. Always a template.

Utility0.0020

Order, booking, payment, and account updates the person expects.

Authentication0.0020

One-time passcodes.

Authentication, international0.1440

A passcode Meta classifies as international.

Service0.0020

A non-template reply inside the 24-hour window, after a free allowance.

Bar length uses the international authentication rate as the full width. From 1 October 2026, the first 1,000 service messages per business phone number per month are free. Further service messages are USD 0.0020. Utility templates are charged whether or not a conversation window is open.

09 · Conclusion

WhatsApp integration is possible. Teams integration is possible.

WhatsApp

The ticketing system can send a template or a text through the Cloud API, and the webhook can read a reply back onto the ticket. Production sending follows business verification, a company number, and approved templates.

Teams

Microsoft Graph can post a direct message to a specific person. The message shows the sender account’s name in that person’s Teams chat. The same notification step chooses Teams when the tenant or the user has selected it.

A status change and an SLA clock can stay quiet, or they can travel on the medium configured for that tenant and that user.
